Coaching Workshop

Coaching Workshop

January 2023

The inaugural Climbing Coaching Workshop in Jordan, the first of its kind, led by renowned Slovenian coaches Klemen Bečan and Borut Kavzar, aimed to develop competitive climbing in the region. This comprehensive training program equipped local coaches with skills in athlete development, training, competition strategies, and mental preparation. The workshop's goal is to prepare the next generation of climbers to compete on the international stage, elevating Jordan's presence in the sport.
