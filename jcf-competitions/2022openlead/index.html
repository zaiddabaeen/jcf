Open Lead Climbing Competition 2022

Open Lead Climbing Competition

25 March 2022

Our second open lead competition and the first competition to include a kids’ category! Congratulations to all the podium winners:

Men’s category: Abdelqader Dabbas in 1st place, Naser Abu Rajeeb in 2nd place and Faisal Al Tajer in 3rd place.

Women’s category: Beesan Halabi in 1st place, Haneen Al Ashram in 2nd place and Zeina Fakhriddin in 3rd place.

Kids category: Yasmeen Wahdan in 1st place, Austin Akers in 2nd place and Yahya Al Alami in 3rd place.

These photos are all courtesy of Ali Barqawi Studios. Feel free to download and use any of them, but do make sure to give the photographer credit!
