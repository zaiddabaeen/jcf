Open Bouldering Competition 2022

Open Bouldering Competition

29-30 July 2022

The first open bouldering competition at Badiya gym. Congratulations to the podium winners!

Adult Male’s category: Osama Qubain in 1st place, Elias Diab in 2nd place and Ali Shareef in 3rd place.

Adult Female’s category: Beesan Halabi in 1st place, Lina Khoury in 2nd place and xxx in 3rd place.

Male youth category: Ivo Tjessem in 1st place, Ghazi Tahabsem in 2nd place and Abdelrahman Al Alami in 3rd place.

Female youth category: Yasmeen Majali in 1st place, Bana Jaradat in 2nd place and Lyla Reaux in 3rd place.

Kids category: Yasmeen Wahdan in 1st place, Yara Qutaishat in 2nd place and Yahya Alalami in 3rd place.
